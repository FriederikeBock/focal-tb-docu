# What you need

In order to revive the old _HLT/EPN_ servers provide to us you will need the following

* 1 Server&#x20;
* 1 Monitor&#x20;
* 2 Powercables: One for the server, one for the monitor&#x20;
* 1 Videocable, e.g. HDMI, Display Port, VGA...&#x20;
* 1 SSD or Harddrive&#x20;
* 1 Keyboard&#x20;
* 1 Mouse&#x20;
* 1 Ethernet cable&#x20;
* 1 laptop with access to the internet&#x20;
* 1 USB stick with at least 1 GB of storage.&#x20;
* a CERN account
